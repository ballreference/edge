How it builds them

A parlay is only worth playing when every leg pays more than its fair price at the same book. The edges then multiply. One bad leg multiplies the book's cut instead, which is why most parlays lose money. This page only combines legs that are positive at the book you'd place it at, and never two legs from the same game, because books price same-game parlays differently. Hit chance is how often it should cash; even a good 4-leg parlay loses most of the time, so bet less on bigger parlays.

Your props are saved in this browser only. Prop prices go stale fast: re-check each leg in the app before you place it.
