How to read this

Each card is a price at one of your books that pays more than the market's fair price, the no-vig line averaged across up to ten books, with Pinnacle and other sharp books counted heavier. Edge is expected profit per $100 if the fair price is right. Only edges of 2.5% or more are flagged, and every flag is logged automatically so the record can't be cherry-picked. Lines move, so check the price in the app before you bet. If it's gone, so is the edge.
